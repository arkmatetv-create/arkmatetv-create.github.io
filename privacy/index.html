Privacy Policy

Arkmate TV privacy information.

Arkmate TV is a player application for macOS. It does not provide media content. Users sign in with their own Xtream credentials.

Data used inside the app

The app stores user-provided Xtream connection details locally on the device when the user chooses to save or restore a session. This data is used only to enable playback, library browsing and session recovery.

Downloads and local storage

If the user uses download or recording features, related files are stored locally in the user-selected or app-supported macOS storage locations.

Support contact

For privacy questions, App Review follow-up or support requests, contact arkmatetv@gmail.com.
